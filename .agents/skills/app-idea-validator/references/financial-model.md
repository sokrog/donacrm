# Financial and market model

## Rules

Use one currency and FX date. Show ranges/formulas and round usefully. Separate one-time, fixed recurring, and variable costs. Verify current fees, taxes, and policies. Downloads are not active users; TAM is not a forecast.

## Market sizing

`TAM users = eligible population × problem incidence × digital/platform reach`

`TAM revenue = TAM users × plausible annual spend`

`reachable users = channel opportunities × click rate × activation rate`

B2B: `target organizations × seats × annual price per seat`

TAM is theoretical demand; SAM applies product/platform/language/region constraints; SOM is plausible within the horizon. Avoid arbitrary “1%” SOM—constrain it by channel reach, budget, conversion, capacity, and competitors.

## Revenue

- Subscription: `active users × paid conversion × net monthly ARPPU`
- Paid download: `paid downloads × net price`
- Ads: `DAU × impressions/day × days × fill rate × eCPM / 1000`
- Marketplace: `transactions × AOV × take rate`
- B2B: `customers × seats × net monthly price`

Model trials, churn/renewal, refunds, fees, taxes, payment loss, incentives, sales cycle, onboarding, and support where relevant.

## Unit economics

`gross profit = net revenue - variable service costs`

`gross margin = gross profit / net revenue`

`CAC = acquisition spend / new activated or paying customers` (state denominator)

`LTV ≈ net ARPPU × gross margin / monthly customer churn`

`payback months = CAC / monthly gross profit per acquired customer`

`break-even month = first month cumulative contribution covers cumulative fixed and one-time costs`

Flag LTV as unstable without retention evidence.

## Costs

Estimate discovery, UX/design, client apps, backend/admin/integrations, QA, accessibility, security, localization, analytics, store preparation, cloud/APIs/AI/maps/messaging/storage, legal/privacy/compliance, support/moderation/operations, ASO/SEO/paid acquisition/creative, maintenance, incidents, and contingency. Tie estimates to MVP scope, staffing, region, and schedule; generic rates are not a quote.

## Scenarios

Use conservative, base, and optimistic cases. Vary acquisition/CAC, activation/paid conversion, price/fees, retention/churn, variable cost, build cost, and delay. Keep combinations coherent. Report MRR, annual net revenue, margin, burn/runway, CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC, payback, break-even, and three most sensitive inputs.
