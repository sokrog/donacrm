# Investor-readiness playbook

## Required narrative

Cover:

1. Company purpose.
2. Problem and current workaround.
3. Solution and durable value proposition.
4. Why now.
5. Target customer, JTBD, buyer, and buying process.
6. Market potential with top-down/bottom-up reconciliation.
7. Direct/indirect alternatives and plan to win.
8. Business model, pricing, margin, and unit economics.
9. Traction: distinguish paid, active, retained, signed, pilot, and pipeline.
10. Go-to-market: channel, funnel, CAC logic, sales cycle, and expansion.
11. Defensibility: data, workflow, network, distribution, IP, brand, or switching costs.
12. Team: founder insight, ability to build/sell, gaps, and hiring.
13. Financial plan: scenario P&L, cash, burn, runway, and milestones.
14. Roadmap and 12–36 month vision.
15. Fundraising ask, use of funds, added runway, and milestone unlocked.

## Input coverage

| Input | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Industry reports/datasets | Market size, growth, macro context |
| Competitor matrix | Positioning, price, saturation, barriers |
| Reviews/rejection reasons | Unmet jobs, trust, switching triggers |
| Interviews/surveys | First-party problem evidence |
| Personas/JTBD | Segment, context, buyer/user distinction |
| Financial model | Economics, capital need, sensitivity |
| Monetization strategy | Revenue mechanism and willingness to pay |
| Existing pitch/business plan | Consistency and unresolved claims |
| MVP specification | Feasibility and learning scope |
| Roadmap | Milestones and capital allocation |
| Product/funnel/cohort data | Traction and retention |
| Team profiles | Founder-market fit and execution risk |
| Fundraising target/use of funds | Investability and milestone logic |
| Legal/IP/cap-table facts | Diligence risk when relevant |

Do not require every item at idea stage. Mark missing proof and convert it into a validation task.

## Pitch-quality tests

- Market, price, acquisition, revenue, costs, and ask use compatible assumptions.
- SOM fits actual channels and budget.
- Traction labels distinguish paid, active, retained, and pipeline.
- The ask maps to dated milestones and runway.
- Risks name thesis-breaking assumptions.
- Every material number has direct provenance.

Beyond the user's document list, explicitly cover why now, traction, go-to-market, defensibility, team, ask/use of funds, milestones, legal/IP risk, and data-room gaps.
